# Philly Blues Website — Weekly Traffic Summary

**Site:** phillybluescfc.com
**Week covered:** Sep 7 – Sep 13, 2026 (last full 7 days)
**Source:** Google Analytics 4
**Prepared:** Sep 14, 2026

---

## The Headlines

A quieter week, which is normal — there were no new launch posts, so this is more of a "steady state" reading.

- **34 people** visited the site (32 of them first-timers).
- They made **79 visits** and viewed the page **84 times**.
- The average visitor spent about **40 seconds** on the page.
- About **35% of visits** were "engaged" (someone who stayed and did more than glance).

These are small numbers, but that's expected in a week with no big announcement. What matters most this week is *where* people came from — and that part is encouraging.

A reminder: our own visits (officers checking the site) are counted here too, so the real outside audience is a bit smaller than 34.

---

## Where the Traffic Came From

Here's how the 79 visits broke down:

- **Google search — 36 visits (46%).** The biggest source this week.
- **Direct — 27 visits (34%).** Typed the address, a saved link, or a link tapped inside an app.
- **Social media — 15 visits (19%).** From social posts.
- **A couple** from the official Chelsea supporters-clubs site.

### The big shift: search is now our #1 source

During launch, social and direct led the way. This week, **Google search moved into first place.** That's a good sign — it means the search-engine setup work (the site being indexed by Google) is starting to pay off, and people are finding us on their own without needing a post to point them there.

### Social breakdown this week

With no new posts, social was quiet and fairly even:

- **X / Twitter — 7 visits**
- **Instagram — 5 visits**
- **Facebook — 3 visits**

Instagram is still our strongest channel over the longer run; it was just quiet this particular week because we didn't post.

---

## The Busiest Day

**Monday, September 7** was the busiest day, with 21 visits. Traffic then eased off through the week — again, the pattern of a week without a new announcement.

---

## Who Was Visiting

- **Location:** Almost entirely US — 97% of visitors (one visitor from Ireland).
- **Device:** **76% on a phone,** 24% on a computer. Still a mobile-first crowd. (Interestingly, the desktop visitors this week stayed much longer — over a minute on average — so the handful of computer users were reading closely.)

---

## 3 Practical Takeaways

1. **Search is quietly working — keep feeding it.** People are now finding us on Google. Keeping the site's text clear and up to date (pub name, match times, "how to join") helps Google send us the right people.

2. **Quiet weeks are quiet for a reason — a post would lift this.** Traffic clearly follows posts. If we want to keep numbers up between big announcements, even a small weekly post (match preview, watch-party reminder) would help.

3. **Still phone-first.** Three out of four visitors are on a phone, so the mobile view remains the one that matters most.

---

## A Few Honest Notes

- This was a low-traffic week by design (no launch posts), so don't read too much into the smaller totals — the *source mix* is the real story.
- Nearly all visitors are still first-timers. As the season builds, watch for **returning visitors** as the sign the site is becoming a habit.
- Google's age/gender data is turned off for this property (a privacy setting), so we can't report visitor demographics.
