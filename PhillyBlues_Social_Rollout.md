# Philly Blues — Social Rollout, 2026/27 Launch

**Angle:** the lucky pub. Cav's was the temporary home in 2020/21. Chelsea won the Champions
League that season. Now it's home for good.

**The wrinkle that makes it work:** Cav's has itself moved one block into a brand new building
with a serious AV setup. So it isn't literally the same room. Don't hide that — it's the
punchline. Neither of you is where you were in 2021, and both of you came out better. Regulars
will spot it anyway, and a joke you make yourself always beats one made at your expense.

Play it dry and let people draw the conclusion.

**Arc:** story → useful → show up.

| # | Post | When | Job |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The lucky pub | Sat 15 – Sun 16 Aug | Announce the return + the site |
| 2 | What changed with tickets | Thu 20 – Fri 21 Aug | Be useful, drive real site traffic |
| 3 | First one back | Sun 23 – Mon 24 Aug | Get bodies in the room |

Post 1 carries the story, so give it the weekend. Post 2 lands as the league kicks off, when
people are already thinking about tickets. Post 3 is the call to action, closest to the match.

# Before you post

**Fix the site hero first.** It currently reads *"a new home pub — and this is our first season
in it."* That contradicts the homecoming story on two counts — it wasn't the first season, and
it isn't quite the same building. Suggested replacement:

> Cav's was our temporary home in 2020/21 — the season Chelsea won the Champions League. It's
> since moved a block and been rebuilt from the ground up. We're back at 1921 Sansom St for
> 2026/27, this time for good.

Same paragraph slot, no layout change needed.

**Update the venue section too.** "A proper room for a proper support" currently says Cav's is
"built for watching football" — which is now literally true and worth saying plainly:

> Cavanaugh's Rittenhouse is a block from where it used to be, in a building finished from
> scratch, with an AV setup that puts most of the city to shame. Every match on, sound up for
> Chelsea, and enough room that the whole group fits without anyone craning at a corner screen.
>
> It's also where we watched the 2021 Champions League run, back when it was a stopgap. Different
> room now. Same idea.

**Fill the photo slots.** Three dashed placeholder boxes are live on the page right now. With
2021 Cav's photos available, use them — they reinforce the exact story the posts are telling.
Caption them "Cav's, 2021" so the connection is explicit for anyone arriving from social. A shot
of the new room alongside them would be even better.

**Check the link preview.** The OG image is currently the crest. Fine, but a 2021 Cav's photo
would preview far better on Facebook, where the image does most of the work.

# Notes

**Tone:** the superstition works because it's underplayed. "Draw your own conclusions" beats
"THE LUCKY PUB IS BACK." Let the reader make the joke — they'll enjoy it more and they're more
likely to reply, which is what actually drives reach.

**Don't cross-post identically.** Each version above is written for its platform. Facebook
tolerates length, Instagram needs the visual to carry it, X gets throttled on links so the hook
has to stand alone.

**Reply to comments in the first hour.** On all three platforms that's the single biggest factor
in how far a post travels, and it matters more than anything in the copy.

**Ask people to bring someone.** The homecoming angle gives lapsed members a reason to come back
— people who drifted after 2021 are exactly the audience this reaches.
